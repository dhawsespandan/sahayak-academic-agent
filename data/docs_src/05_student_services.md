# Student Services and Certificates (Sample Regulations for Sahayak Prototype)

## 1. Bonafide Certificate
A bonafide certificate is issued for purposes such as bank accounts, internships, passports and scholarships. Requests are made through the student helpdesk with the purpose stated clearly. The certificate is ready for collection from the administrative office within 3 working days.

## 2. Internship No-Objection Certificate (NOC)
Students of the one-year internship batch who need a NOC for an internship must submit the offer letter to the faculty mentor. The NOC is issued by the Training and Placement cell within 5 working days after the mentor's recommendation.

## 3. ID Card Replacement
A lost ID card can be replaced by applying at the administrative office with a fee of Rs. 200. The new card is issued within 2 working days.

## 4. Grievance Redressal
Academic grievances that are not resolved by the faculty mentor within 7 working days can be escalated to the Head of Department, and thereafter to the Student Grievance Committee.

## 5. Helpdesk Hours
The administrative office and student helpdesk are open from 9:30 AM to 4:30 PM, Monday to Friday. The Sahayak assistant is available at all times, but requests submitted after office hours are processed on the next working day.

## 6. Contact
For questions not answered by Sahayak, students may contact their faculty mentor. Contact details of mentors are available on the student portal.
