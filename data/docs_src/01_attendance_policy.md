# Attendance Policy (Sample Regulations for Sahayak Prototype)

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to all students enrolled in undergraduate programmes, including the one-year internship batch (Division C), for every theory and laboratory course in the semester.

## 2. Minimum Attendance Requirement
A student must maintain a minimum of 75 percent attendance in each course separately. Attendance is calculated on the total number of lectures planned for the semester in that course, not only on the lectures conducted so far. A student whose attendance in a course falls below 75 percent at the end of the semester is not eligible to appear for the end-semester examination of that course.

## 3. Calculation of Attendance
Attendance percentage = (lectures attended / lectures planned for the semester) x 100. The planned lecture count for every course is published in the course plan in the first week of the semester. Mid-semester figures shown on the portal are computed on lectures conducted till date and are only indicative.

## 4. Condonation of Shortage
The Head of Department may condone a shortage of attendance of up to 10 percent (that is, down to 65 percent) only on medical grounds or for representing the institute in approved events. The student must submit supporting documents (medical certificate or event letter) within 7 calendar days of rejoining classes. Shortage below 65 percent cannot be condoned under any circumstances.

## 5. Detention
A student who is not eligible due to attendance shortage is marked "Detained" for that course and must re-register for the course in the next available offering.

## 6. Monitoring and Alerts
Faculty mentors review attendance every two weeks. Students below 80 percent in any course receive an early warning so that they can plan to attend the remaining lectures.

## 7. Attendance for Interns
Students of the one-year internship batch attending classes in online or hybrid mode must join the live session; recorded-lecture viewing is not counted as attendance.
