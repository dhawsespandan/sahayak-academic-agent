# Continuous Assessment and Evaluation Scheme (Sample Regulations for Sahayak Prototype)

## 1. Components of Evaluation
Each theory course is evaluated for 100 marks: 40 marks of Continuous Assessment (CA) and 60 marks of End-Semester Examination (ESE).

## 2. Continuous Assessment Structure
The 40 CA marks are distributed as follows: CA1 (written test) 10 marks, CA2 (assignment or quiz) 10 marks, and CA3 (mini project with report) 20 marks.

## 3. Passing Criteria
A student must score at least 40 percent in CA (16 out of 40) and at least 40 percent in ESE (24 out of 60), and at least 40 percent overall (40 out of 100), to pass a course.

## 4. Publication of CA Marks
CA marks are published on the student portal within 10 working days of the assessment. The publication date is shown along with the marks.

## 5. Re-evaluation of CA Marks
A student who is not satisfied with CA marks may request re-evaluation within 5 working days (Monday to Friday) of the date on which those marks were published. Requests received after this window are not accepted. Re-evaluation is done by the course faculty and the marks may increase, decrease or remain the same. The decision of the course faculty is final for CA components.

## 6. Make-up Assessment
A student who misses CA1 or CA2 on approved medical grounds may request one make-up assessment through the faculty mentor within 7 calendar days of rejoining. No make-up is allowed for CA3.

## 7. Academic Integrity
Plagiarism or use of unfair means in any CA component results in zero marks for that component and may lead to disciplinary action. Use of generative AI tools in CA3 reports must be declared in the report.
