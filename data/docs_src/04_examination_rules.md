# End-Semester Examination Rules (Sample Regulations for Sahayak Prototype)

## 1. Eligibility
Only students who meet the attendance requirement (75 percent per course, or condoned down to 65 percent) and who have submitted all CA components are eligible to appear for the end-semester examination.

## 2. Hall Ticket
Hall tickets are issued on the student portal 7 days before the first examination. A student must carry a printed hall ticket and college ID card to every examination. Students without a hall ticket are not allowed into the examination hall.

## 3. Reporting Time
Students must report to the examination hall 30 minutes before the start of the paper. No student is allowed to enter after 30 minutes from the start of the examination, and no student may leave the hall during the first 60 minutes.

## 4. Prohibited Items
Mobile phones, smart watches, earphones and any electronic device other than a permitted non-programmable calculator are strictly prohibited inside the examination hall. Possession of a prohibited item is treated as use of unfair means.

## 5. Unfair Means
Cases of unfair means are reported to the Examination Committee. Penalties range from cancellation of the paper to cancellation of the entire semester examination.

## 6. Missed Examination
A student who misses an end-semester examination on medical grounds may appear in the supplementary examination. The application with a medical certificate must be submitted within 3 working days of the missed paper.

## 7. Results
Results are declared within 30 days of the last examination. Photocopy of answer books can be requested within 5 working days of the result declaration.
