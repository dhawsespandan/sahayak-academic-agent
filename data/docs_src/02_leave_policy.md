# Leave Policy for Students (Sample Regulations for Sahayak Prototype)

## 1. Types of Leave
Students may apply for (a) personal leave, (b) medical leave, and (c) duty leave for representing the institute in approved academic, cultural or sports events.

## 2. Approving Authority
Leave of up to 3 consecutive days is approved by the faculty mentor of the division. Leave of more than 3 consecutive days must be forwarded by the faculty mentor and approved by the Head of Department (HoD).

## 3. Supporting Documents
Medical leave of more than 2 days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner. Duty leave requires an official letter from the event organiser or the faculty in charge. Personal leave does not require documents but must state a clear reason.

## 4. Timeline for Applying
Personal leave must be applied for at least 1 working day in advance. Medical leave may be applied for after the fact, but the application and certificate must reach the faculty mentor within 7 calendar days of rejoining classes.

## 5. Effect of Leave on Attendance
Approved leave does not add attendance. Lectures missed during approved leave are still counted as absent. However, approved medical leave and duty leave are considered by the HoD when deciding condonation of attendance shortage under the Attendance Policy.

## 6. Maximum Leave
A student may not take more than 15 days of personal leave in a semester. Leave during the end-semester examination period is not permitted except on medical grounds.

## 7. How to Apply
Leave applications are submitted through the student helpdesk (Sahayak) or in writing to the faculty mentor. Each application receives a reference number which the student should quote in any follow-up.
